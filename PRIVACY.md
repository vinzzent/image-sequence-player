**Privacy Policy – Image Sequence Player**

**Overview**
This custom visual for Microsoft Power BI does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal data.

**Local Processing & Data Residency**
All processing occurs locally in Power BI Desktop or in the browser (Power BI Service). No data is exported outside the user’s Power BI tenant.

**External Image Loading (Optional)**
The visual can operate using only local data (e.g., inline SVGs, Base64, or as a metadata orchestrator). If external image URLs are used:

* Requests are made directly by the user’s Power BI environment.
* The developer has no access to URLs, requests, or image content.
* External servers may log requests (e.g., IP addresses) under their own policies. Users are responsible for ensuring compliance with their organization’s data policies.

**License Verification**
License status is verified using Microsoft’s native licensing API. This process occurs within Microsoft’s infrastructure, and no personal data or telemetry is shared with the developer.

**No Tracking or External Services**
The visual does not use cookies, analytics, telemetry, or third-party tracking. The developer operates no external systems that interact with the visual or its users.

**Policy Updates**
This policy may be updated to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. Updates will be posted here.
